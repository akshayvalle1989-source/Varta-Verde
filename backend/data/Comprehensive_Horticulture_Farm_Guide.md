#### Subsidy Pattern: 

- General Category: 40% of equipment cost (max ₹40,000)

#### Application Process: 

1. Register on State DBT Agriculture Portal 

2. Submit: Land ownership proof + Aadhar 

3. Selection through draw of lots (merit + lottery system) 

4. Equipment purchase from authorized dealer 

5. Subsidy released in 2 installments via Direct Benefit Transfer 

Timeline: 60-90 days processing 

#### 2. Agricultural Infrastructure Fund (AIF) 

Loan Amount: Up to ₹1 crore per project 

#### Eligible Infrastructure: 

Drip/micro-irrigation systems 

- Cold chain infrastructure 

- Storage facilities 

- Processing units 

- Greenhouses/poly houses 

Interest Rate: PM-FME Scheme linked (currently 5-7%) 

#### Repayment Period: 10 years 

Subsidy: 2% interest subsidy for 5 years (government contribution) 

Application: Through nearest bank (NABARD coordinated) 

#### 3. PM-KUSUM (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha uttam Mahabhiyojana) 

Applicable For: Irrigation equipment powered by solar 

Components: 

Component A: Solar pumps for agriculture 

Component C: Grid-connected solar plants on farm land 

Subsidy: 30% of project cost (General), 50% (SC/ST/Women) Benefit: Reduced electricity costs (85% savings possible)
